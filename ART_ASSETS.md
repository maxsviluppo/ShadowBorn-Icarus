# Nuove pose del personaggio — v2

Strumento: image_gen integrato (skill imagegen), non CLI/API. Asset copiati nel progetto:
- public/assets/character/turn-source.png: generazione originale, RGBA.
- public/assets/character/turn.png: atlante tecnico 640×768, celle 160×192, sagome alte 144 pixel, piedi a quota 186.
- scripts/pack-turns.mjs: ritaglio e impaginazione riproducibile, senza ridisegno.

Riferimenti: idle.png originale di Shadowborn per identità e abbigliamento; atlante N.png per vista posteriore e zaino. La cella 7 guarda troppo verso il retro; non è usata, sostituita in quel settore dalla posa laterale. Non si presume che una generazione garantisca angoli geometricamente esatti.

Prompt completo utilizzato:

Create a production game sprite atlas of the SAME character in reference image 1, with reference 2 only describing his brown leather backpack and rear appearance. Preserve exactly the youthful male adventurer's identity, tousled golden hair, blue eyes, green diamond-quilted tunic, cream sleeves, brown belt and side pouch, brown trousers, brown boots and brown backpack. Match the hand-painted crisp outlined 2D adventure game style. Output a single 2048x2048 PNG with REAL transparent alpha background. EXACTLY 16 full-body standing poses in a perfectly regular 4 columns x 4 rows grid of 512x512 cells. Each figure same 390 pixel height, centered horizontally in its own cell, soles at 470 pixels within its cell. Same relaxed slightly slouching neutral stance with both feet close together and arms relaxed, NOT walking. They are successive views of one stationary character rotating in place clockwise through 360 degrees, viewed from a fixed moderately elevated isometric camera. Read row-major left-to-right: frame 0 faces screen right (E), 1 faces right-and-slightly-down (ESE), 2 faces diagonally down-right (SE), 3 faces mostly down slight right (SSE); row 2: 4 faces directly toward viewer (S), 5 SSW, 6 SW, 7 WSW; row 3: 8 faces screen left (W), 9 WNW, 10 NW, 11 NNW; row 4: 12 faces directly away from viewer (N, backpack), 13 NNE, 14 NE, 15 ENE. Angle step exactly 22.5 degrees. Camera, anatomy, head size and clothes fixed across all cells. Feet stay grounded at identical baseline. Include rear views and profiles, not only front views. Absolutely NO ground shadow, NO white ellipse, NO halo, NO floor, NO cast shadow, NO rim light, NO cell boundaries, NO labels, NO text, NO extra objects. Transparent space between separate silhouettes, no touching or overlapping figures. This sheet is for smooth in-place turns, not a walking cycle.
