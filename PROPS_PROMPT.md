## Prompt completo

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production sprite asset sheet containing exactly TWO isolated pieces of medieval wooden furniture for the illustrated isometric adventure room in the reference screenshot. Reference is for style, colors and camera only, do NOT redraw the screenshot or user interface. Real transparent alpha background, no ground, no shadows outside the objects, no text, no labels, no interface. Landscape sheet, two equal columns with wide transparent separation. LEFT: a beautiful rustic rectangular oak writing table, completely EMPTY tabletop, four sturdy turned wooden legs, subtle carved apron, warm walnut/oak grain, worn bevelled edges, small iron joinery. RIGHT: a closed medieval oak storage chest, low slightly arched lid, forged dark iron bands and rivets, small aged brass lock, stout wooden feet, charming hand-painted craftsmanship. Match the room's outlined hand-painted 2D adventure-game illustration, restrained warm brown palette, warm light from upper left, shaded right faces, modest detail readable at small sprite size. Both are viewed from the same orthographic isometric camera, 2:1 ground axes (visible top edges slope about 26.5 degrees). The long axis of each object runs from upper-left to lower-right on the screen, and its short axis from upper-right to lower-left. Full silhouettes visible, nothing cropped. Each object entirely inside its own half of the canvas. Table legs must all make sense structurally and the tabletop must have the same coherent isometric perspective as the room. Chest is lower and squat. Do not include keys, papers, cups, people or any extra objects. Transparent space between legs and around both props. Keep all pixels outside the furniture actually transparent, not a checkerboard pattern.
